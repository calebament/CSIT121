This is the first page I have done on my own, and even though some parts were confusing to me, there were some things I used to make it easier. The notes I took and the lessons from the textbook went a long way in helping me, and having a sheet full of the HTML tags is going to help me through the semester. Lab 3 Reflection

For lab 3, I definitely had to go through a little more trouble to figure out how everything from the CSS sheet is referenced, but now that I have it figured out, it immediately makes everything more visually appealing. Even if certain things such as the class selector that I went with isn't entirely necessary, its still useful to know for any future labs. Lab 6 Reflection

Lab 6 has been by far the most challenging, but now that the learning process is done, it seems less daunting than it actually felt. It took me the longest of all of them, but ended up being the greatest learning experience. One of the biggest takeaways I got from this lab was the importance of creating a responsive and well designed experience for computers, phones, and tablets of all kinds, which is one of the most important things to consider in best design practices. As well as this, it is also important to consider how the interactions with the UI influence the user's experience, such as clear visual feedback and a pleasant, understandable UI. Overall, I am glad that I was able to learn from this in a way that will help me advance in this class.
