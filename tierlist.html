Reflection

While this was not as challenging as the previous lab, I thought this was the most fun lab to do so far. I went with a tier list for Bloodborne because it is one of my favorite games and the amount of bosses was perfect for this size of a tier list. Certain parts of this lab felt somewhat tedious, but one thing I have very much learned in this class is how useful it is to copy some of the more monotonous bits from previous assignments. I feel confident using both tables and forms now and see how important it is for designing a website.
