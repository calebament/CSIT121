What is this PNG?

This PNG is a render of Solid Snake from the Metal Gear video game series. It depicts him holding his hand to his earpiece.

What is a PNG?

PNG is short for Portable Network Graphic. It supports 16 million colors and is well supported across all major browsers. It is a lossless image format and supports multiple levels of transparency.

Why Did I Choose This Image?

For the purpose of showing the benefits of each image format, I chose a PNG that shows the levels of transparency that it offers. I like this image in particular because Solid Snake is one of my favorite characters.
