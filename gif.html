What is this GIF?

This GIF is a short loop of animation from the series The Midnight Gospel. It shows the main character of the series, Clancy, listening to music.

What is a GIF?

GIF is short for Graphics Interchange Format. They can only display 256 colors, which is much smaller than many other formats, but it has the benefit of being able to display animations. It is a lossless format and allows one color to be transparent.

Why Did I Choose This Image?

For the purpose of showing the benefits of each image format, I wanted to choose a GIF with an animation to show off what makes GIFs special. I also wanted to inject a little bit of my own personality into it, so I picked something from one of my favorite shows.
